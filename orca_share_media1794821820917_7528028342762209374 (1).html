How to read the labels

Extinct (EX)
No reasonable doubt that the last individual has died.
Critically Endangered (CR)
Extremely high risk of extinction in the wild.
Endangered (EN)
Very high risk of extinction in the wild.
Vulnerable (VU)
High risk of extinction in the wild.
IUCN vs DENR
IUCN categories are global. Philippine lists (DAO 2019-09 for animals, DAO 2017-11 for plants) apply under RA 9147 inside the country and do not automatically copy the IUCN list, so the two can differ.
